### Pink Spotlight
A soft pink spotlight behind my head and a clean white search card.
*Needs cutout.*
- Background and light: Cut me out of my background. Behind me, a radial pink glow centred just above my head (#ff9fcb fading to a warm beige). A soft pink rim light on one side of me and a faint pink glow around my outline.
- Text and graphics: A white card with rounded corners, tilted -1.5 degrees, above my head, showing the steps I'm talking about. The field I'm on gets a hot-pink ring. When I finish, the card flips to the result in a big pink italic serif.
- Captions: Dark pill (90% black) in Inter ExtraBold white. Key words in Instrument Serif Italic, pink, a bit bigger.
- 16:9 and 4:3: Me on the right two-thirds, the card on the left, same pink spotlight behind me.

### Signature Pink
The look of my first viral-ideas video.
*No cutout needed.*
- Background and light: Keep my real room. Add a gradient over the wall that fades from black at the top into deep magenta, then into my wall around my shoulders. No cut-out needed.
- Text and graphics: Show the app on screen as a dark rounded card (like X's own sheets) above my head. On key lines, blur me out (45px blur, 55% darker) and put the card in the middle. Values type in as I say them, in hot pink #FF2E93. Add a pink 'VIRAL ONLY' stamp that slams in, rotated -7 degrees. A handwritten note in Caveat on a white tag for small asides.
- Captions: Black pill (95% opacity, fully rounded) in Bricolage Grotesque Bold, white. Key words switch to Instrument Serif Italic in hot pink. Words I haven't said yet are 55% white.
- 16:9 and 4:3: Split screen: graphics on a dark magenta-to-black stage on the left, my vertical video full height on the right (608px wide on 1920), with a 3px hot-pink line between them. Captions centred under the graphics.
