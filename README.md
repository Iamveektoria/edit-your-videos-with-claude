# Edit Your Videos With Claude

This gives Claude 30 ready-made video styles for your talking-head clips, plus a raw-footage cleanup tool. No editing software, no timeline, no learning curve. You upload a video and tell Claude what you want.

## Before you start

You need the Claude app (iPhone, Android, or desktop) with Code access. This is a paid feature, not the free plan. Look for a "Code" tab or "coding" option when you open the app. If you only see a plain chat box with no file or coding ability, you don't have the right access yet.

## Step 1: make your own copy

Click the green "Use this template" button at the top of this page, then "Create a new repository." Give it any name, such as "my-video-edits," leave everything else as-is, and create it. This makes you a personal copy. Nothing you do here affects anyone else's copy, including mine.

## Step 2: open it in Claude

Open the Claude app and start a new Code (or "coding") session. When it asks which project or repository, connect your GitHub account if you haven't already, then pick the repository you just created in Step 1. Wait for the session to open. This can take a minute the first time.

## Step 3: see the styles

Type this:

/edit-video styles

Claude shows you a page with all 30 styles. Under each style's preview is its own command in a little box, like this:

/edit-video Word Pop

That's the exact text you paste to use that style. Every style has a different one, named after that style.

## Step 4: use a style on your video

Attach your video to the chat (the same way you'd attach a photo in any chat app), then paste that style's command, for example:

/edit-video Word Pop

Claude will ask a few quick questions (which sizes you want, colours, music), then transcribe your video and point out any filler words or repeated takes to cut. After that, it shows you a storyboard preview to approve before rendering anything. Nothing gets rendered until you say it looks good.

If you already know the style name, you don't need to see the gallery first. You can paste its command straight away, right after attaching your video.

## Cleaning up raw footage without a style

To just cut filler words, dead air, and repeated takes, without putting it in a style, type this instead:

/edit-video clean

## If something looks stuck

Renders can take a few minutes. That's normal. If it stops responding, just say "please continue."
